# Onboard a growth workspace

Create a useful, resumable starting point rather than a long questionnaire. Coordinate existing records and workflows; do not create a parallel customer profile or claim that a proposal is an agreed growth strategy.

Read [workspace format](references/workspace-format.md) before creating or changing records. It defines ownership, provenance, research, goals, initiatives, and resume checks.

## Discover and resume

Read project instructions, customer entry documents, the resource index, setup and tools records, business context, glossary, existing strategies, research, decisions, progress, and recent evidence. Follow relevant remote links through available connectors. Resolve relative paths from the containing document, not the installed skill folder.

Summarize what is already known, what conflicts, and the first unresolved dependency. Reconcile saved claims with current records and customer edits. Missing access is not proof of absence; keep an inaccessible authoritative record linked and continue only independent work.

If an onboarding record or progress entry exists, resume it. Do not restart completed sections, repeat confirmed questions, or create replacement records because a session or assistant changed.

## Confirm the working arrangement

Reuse the setup record when present. Use analytics-setup when installed for missing tools, platforms, work locations, permission defaults, workflows in play, or conventions. When it is unavailable, collect only the storage, permissions, and tool facts needed for onboarding and mark the remaining setup open.

Confirm the authoritative storage location and verify relevant read and write access before relying on it. For a new local workspace with no suitable existing location, propose `.agents/growth/` and confirm it. Keep customer records outside installed skill folders. An accessible local draft is not a remotely published record.

Ask at most three unresolved questions per round. Allow deferral and incomplete answers; save useful progress between rounds. Production changes, external messages, paid actions, tracker creation, and schedules require their own authorization.

## Build sourced business context

Read available product documents and actual project evidence before asking. Extend the existing business context and glossary through strategy when installed, or use their established native format without changing ownership. Capture only what growth planning needs: offering and value, customers and accounts, journeys, business model, owners, constraints, and relevant market terms.

Use public research to supplement rather than overwrite customer knowledge. Search the business and its market with available web tools; prefer direct company, product, pricing, customer, and official market sources. Record source URLs, publishers, retrieval dates, supported claims, and limitations. Distinguish current facts from old or promotional claims.

Show a concise sourced summary for correction. Apply the five provenance states in the reference to every material claim. Never turn a plausible inference into a customer-confirmed fact.

## Understand competitors and alternatives

Start with competitors and alternatives the customer names, including direct products, indirect substitutes, and doing nothing when relevant. Add research candidates only with evidence and a distinct candidate status. Record why each belongs, the segment or job compared, sources, and uncertainty.

Do not treat search rankings, shared keywords, or similar descriptions as sufficient proof of competition. Do not invent market share, positioning, pricing, or competitive advantage. Leave comparison cells unknown when evidence is missing.

## Establish goals and evidence

Use the existing strategy for each goal. Record the desired outcome, customer or segment, decision supported, baseline, target, unit, timeframe, owner, guardrails, and measurement gaps. Preserve agreed, proposed, and deferred states. Unknown baselines, targets, dates, and owners remain unknown.

Inspect available documents, data, and tool capabilities before requesting new tracking or a provider. Distinguish an observed connection from read access, write access, and an untested capability. Never request credentials for deferred tools or make a paid provider call during onboarding.

When a goal needs data, offer feasible routes among already connected tools, customer-owned exports, direct third-party access, public research, and any managed capability actually available in the host. Explain access, cost, and freshness trade-offs that are known; mark the others for evaluation. Do not promise an unavailable integration.

## Propose initial initiatives

Create a short, prioritized work plan in the strategy, linked to its goals and evidence. For each initiative record the expected outcome, impact, confidence, effort, dependencies, required capability, owner when known, and proposed or agreed status. Explain meaningful uncertainty and dependencies instead of producing false numerical precision.

Prefer initiatives that resolve a material evidence gap or advance a confirmed goal. A competitor list or completed profile is not itself a growth outcome. Do not create tracker tasks, launch campaigns, contact providers, or start monitoring without separate authorization.

## Save, verify, and hand back

Save progressively to the recorded locations. Preserve stable IDs, customer edits, unrelated sections, and existing links. Read back affected fields and links when possible; after an ambiguous write, look for the existing record before retrying. Record an unavailable readback or partial write explicitly.

Maintain one progress section in the resource index or its linked progress record. Link completed records, open choices, research gaps, blockers and their owners, and one next action. Keep provenance and verification limits visible.

Return the resource index, business context, research, goals, and initiative links that actually exist. Summarize confirmed facts, researched candidates, important unknowns, and the next action. Do not claim onboarding is complete merely because files were created; name any unconfirmed choices or inaccessible sources.
